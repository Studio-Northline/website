About this app

CodeSnap is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator that works entirely on your phone. Point the camera at any code and it reads it right away, or pick a photo from your gallery when the code sits inside an image.

The scanner covers the formats you run into every day: QR codes plus EAN-8, EAN-13, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, ITF, PDF417, Codabar, Data Matrix, and Aztec. There is a flashlight for dark places, and every scan lands in your history so you can open it again later.

The generator handles text, URLs, Wi-Fi networks (open networks included), contacts, email, phone numbers, SMS, and locations. Customize the colors, export the result in HD, and share or print it. Premium lifts the limits on customization, history, and export quality, and removes ads.

No account, no sign-up, and nothing you scan ever leaves your phone. Scanning and generating happen on the device, so both work with no internet connection. The app follows your system theme, comes in 11 languages, and shows ads unless you go Premium.

Privacy Policy for CodeSnap

1. What we don't collect

CodeSnap is a scanner and generator app. To be clear about what it does not do:

  • No account or sign-up: there is no registration, login, or user profile.
  • No collection of personal data by the app itself: we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts, and we don't upload the content you scan or generate.
  • No sale of personal data. We don't sell or rent data about you to anyone.
  • Your scan and generation history is stored only on your device, in local app storage. You can delete it at any time from the app's history screen, or by clearing the app's data in system settings.

Some data is processed by third-party services the app integrates (advertising and crash analytics), as described below.

2. Camera

The camera permission is used only for live scanning: frames are decoded in real time to find and read QR codes and barcodes.

  • Camera frames are processed on your device.
  • Frames are never recorded to video or stored as images.
  • Frames are never transmitted off your device for scanning purposes.

The flashlight toggle only controls the camera flash for scanning in low light.

3. Images (gallery scans and exports)

When you choose to decode a code from an image in your gallery, or to export/save a generated code as an image file, that image is processed locally on your device. Image contents aren't uploaded to our servers. CodeSnap has no backend servers that receive your images.

4. Advertising

CodeSnap shows ads, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID and device/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Google Play settings.

See Google's privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Buying Premium removes ads, but it doesn't change the data practices described here beyond no longer requesting ads.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

CodeSnap uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash and diagnostic data, for example stack traces, device/OS version, and the app version at the time of a crash. That data is used only to find and fix bugs. It isn't used to identify you personally.

See Google Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Children

CodeSnap isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service the app uses, email us and we'll take care of it.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent by email to northline.studio.developer@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version always lives on this page. The effective date above tells you when this version started applying.

1. Who we are

Spot the Difference is an Android app made by Northline Studio, an indie developer. We're the people behind it, and we're the ones answering for how it behaves.

Every privacy question — access, correction, deletion, or just a straight "what's happening here?" — goes to the same address: northline.studio.developer@gmail.com. There's only one of us, so there's no switchboard to work out.

2. Information we don't collect

The app has no registration, so there's no account, no name, no email address and no phone number sitting anywhere. We don't ask for your contacts, we don't read your photos or media library, and we don't ask for your location.

The app requests exactly two Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — for ads, for purchases, and for the links that open the website or the Play Store.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — so the app can check whether it's online before it tries to load an ad or a purchase. It tells us you're connected or not, nothing more.

No camera. No microphone. No contacts. No files. No location. Nothing is scanned, recorded or read off your phone.

3. Information stored on your device

Everything the game remembers lives on your phone and nowhere else:

  • Gameplay progress — the levels you've finished and the stars you've earned
  • Your coins, hints and hearts
  • Your settings, including High contrast
  • The timers behind daily rewards

None of it is uploaded to a Northline Studio server, because there isn't one. It stays inside Android's app sandbox until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

4. Advertising

The app is ad-supported. It shows banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint or some hearts.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, or by an equivalent ad network once ads go live. Like most ad-funded apps, ads may use your Advertising ID. That's governed by Google's policies and by your device's advertising settings — you can reset it or switch off personalized ads from Google Play settings.

Rewarded ads are always optional and always labelled. Nothing in the game is locked behind one. A one-time Remove Ads purchase switches off banners and interstitials; rewarded ads stay available, because watching one is always your choice. Purchases are processed by Google Play, so we never see your payment details.

If you're in a region that requires it, a consent form appears on the first launch, before any advertising or analytics request is made.

See Google's privacy policy for the details of how AdMob handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Optional Google Play Games sign-in

You can sign in with Google Play Games to save your progress to your Play Games profile and to take part in leaderboards. It's entirely optional.

Without it, everything stays local on your device and the game is fully playable — no level, star or feature is held back behind a sign-in. If you do sign in, Google handles your Play Games data under Google's own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Analytics

We collect a handful of anonymous gameplay events: when a level starts, when it's completed, the app version, a random identifier created at install, and whether the device was online at the time.

That data is never tied to an identity, never sold, and never used to advertise to you. It exists so we can tell which levels are too hard and whether a feature is being used.

No crash-reporting SDK is included in this app. We don't collect crash logs, stack traces or device diagnostics.

7. Children

Spot the Difference doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone below the age at which your local law lets them consent on their own.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, email the address in section 1 and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Write to northline.studio.developer@gmail.com and we'll answer.

A couple of things in the app open somewhere else: links use Chrome Custom Tabs to show our website pages, and the store button opens Google Play. Those are run by Google, so Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to them.

9. Data retention and security

Your progress sits on your device under Android's app sandbox, which other apps can't read. Uninstalling the app removes it, and so does clearing the app's data in system settings. Nothing about your play is kept on our side.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and move the effective date at the top, so you can always tell which version you're reading.
